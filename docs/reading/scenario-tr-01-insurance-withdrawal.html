TR-01-insurance-withdrawal

Firm scenario card

Type: transmission (institutional)

Physical driver: Optional — often stacked on STD-01 or STD-02

Mechanism

Capacity exit or sublimits in high-risk geographies → higher retentions / uncovered damage → faster credit tightening or exit cap expansion → forced management action.

Precedent

Coastal property insurance non-renewals and residual market migration in parts of Florida and California (public reporting patterns).

Application

  • If asset already exclusions_flood / in_market_review / uninsured: bump severity +1 (cap S3).
  • If clean full cover: apply +25–50% to residual loss after physical damage fraction (judgment band).

Decision relevance

Often changes the ROI of mitigation more than the physical scenario alone.
