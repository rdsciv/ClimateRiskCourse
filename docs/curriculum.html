Curriculum

Three phases: the client, the analysis, the delivery. Two live-session rhythm by design; self-paced works the same arc.

Simulated training data only. Numbers you compute in the repo are educational — not real client advice.
Week Question Deliverable Exercises
0 · Orientation How do I take this course? Environment ready; track chosen 00-orientation
1 · The Client What does this client need, and how will you deliver it? Initial Framing & Delivery Plan 01-the-client

6 · Delivery Can this stand up in front of the board and the regulator? Board Presentation + Regulatory Submission 06-delivery

What you’ll learn (by phase)

Week 1

Client workspace

Grok project rules, engagement AGENTS.md, querying the book, scoping materiality, delivery plan.

Weeks 2–5

Analysis chain

Geocode & classify, hazard joins, standard + bespoke scenarios, multi-agent runs, decision language.

Week 6

Two audiences

Judgment for the board. Record for the regulator. Figure lineage and QA agents.

Weekly rhythm

  • Monday pattern — methodology against the live environment; leave with a working pattern.
  • Mid-week — 3–4 hours of build time on your client track; optional check-in.
  • Friday — demo the deliverable; compare approaches across tracks.

Self-paced: treat each section as a week. Do not skip the audit trail.
