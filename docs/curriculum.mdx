Three phases: **the client**, **the analysis**, **the delivery**.

## Map

| Week | Question | Deliverable | Exercises |
|------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **0 · Orientation** | How do I take this course? | Environment ready; track chosen | `00-orientation` |
| **1 · The Client** | What does this client need? | Framing & delivery plan | `01-the-client` |
| **2 · Mapping** | Where does risk concentrate? | Mapped portfolio + hypotheses | `02-portfolio-mapping` |
| **3 · Hazard** | Which hazard data matters? | Organized hazard dataset | `03-hazard-data` |
| **4 · Scenarios** | Which scenarios bite? | Standard + bespoke results | `04-scenario-analysis` |
| **5 · Synthesis** | What should change? | Portfolio synthesis | `05-portfolio-synthesis` |
| **6 · Delivery** | Will it stand up? | Board + record pack | `06-delivery` |
