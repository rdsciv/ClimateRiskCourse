Three client tracks

Pick the world closest to your work. Stay with one track through the capstone.

ironwood

Ironwood Bank

U.S. commercial bank, subsidiary of a Frankfurt-based group. Tiered data quality, messy questionnaires, contradictory disclosures.

  • Scale: ~100 counterparties (training book)
  • Decision language: credit pricing, capital, limits
  • Audiences: CRO, board, parent risk, regulator dry-run
  • Folder: clients/ironwood-bank/
strata

Strata Holdings

Diversified real estate and infrastructure fund. Site-level physical risk mapped to acquisition and exit pricing.

  • Scale: ~45 assets
  • Decision language: acquisition / hold / exit, insurance transmission
  • Audiences: IC, LPs, risk
  • Folder: clients/strata-holdings/
northwood

Northwood Capital

Mid-cap industrial with climate-exposed supply chain spanning multiple continents.

  • Scale: ~60 facilities / supplier nodes
  • Decision language: contingency, dual-source, input disruption
  • Audiences: COO, procurement, exec ops
  • Folder: clients/northwood-capital/

Activate a track

python3 scripts/set_track.py ironwood   # or strata | northwood
python3 scripts/seed_all_clients.py     # once after clone

All client data is simulated and labeled as such in briefings and meta tables.
