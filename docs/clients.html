Three client tracks

Pick the world closest to your work. Stay with one track through the capstone. All books are simulated training data.

colorado

Redrock Basin Authority

Colorado River–style multi-reservoir operations under drought and heat. Storage, diversions, deliveries, hydropower, and environmental pulse nodes.

  • Scale: ~48 system nodes
  • Decision language: releases, allocations, compact-sensitive deliveries, hydropower contingency
  • Audiences: GM, municipal/ag boards, compact counsel
  • Folder: clients/colorado-river-reservoirs/
kerrville

City of Kerrville — flood risk

Municipal flood resilience along the Guadalupe / Hill Country corridor (Texas). Critical facilities, neighborhoods, and access infrastructure.

  • Scale: ~80 facilities & parcels (simulated)
  • Decision language: mitigation priority, access, buyout vs defend
  • Audiences: city manager, emergency management, finance/council
  • Folder: clients/kerrville-flood/
datacenter

Horizon Grid LLC — Texas data center EIS

Environmental impact statement support for a fictional Texas hyperscale campus: pads, water options, power, transmission, and community receptors.

  • Scale: ~55 EIS elements across alternatives
  • Decision language: alternatives analysis, water, grid, receptors, mitigation
  • Audiences: project board, environmental counsel, community liaison
  • Folder: clients/texas-datacenter-eis/

Activate a track

uv run scripts/set_track.py colorado   # or kerrville | datacenter
uv run scripts/seed_all_clients.py     # once after clone

All data is simulated. Real place names (e.g. Kerrville) anchor geography; figures and asset books are training constructs.
