# Scenario cards

Each card is a reusable stress narrative with:

- **ID** and name  
- **Type:** standard (for the record) or transmission (institutional)  
- **Physical driver** (if any)  
- **Mechanism** of financial impact  
- **Precedent** (named historical or well-documented market episode)  
- **Parameters** students/agents may vary in break-point tests  

Cards are starting points. Bespoke scenarios in week 4 combine cards with portfolio hypotheses.
